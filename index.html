Key Ingredients

Consolidated ingredient profile used across the LEM line.

  • Carbonated water
  • Natural caffeine (green tea, green coffee, or guarana)
  • L-Carnitine
  • B-Vitamins (B3, B5, B6, B12)
  • L-Theanine
  • Herbal & fruit extracts (ginseng, acai, guarana, lavender)
  • Natural flavors (juice concentrates, extracts, oils)
  • Sweeteners: Stevia and/or Monk Fruit
  • Acidulant: Citric acid
  • Preservatives: Potassium sorbate, Sodium benzoate
